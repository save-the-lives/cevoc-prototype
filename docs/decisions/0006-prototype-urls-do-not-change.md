# 0006. Existing prototype URLs must not change

Status: Accepted
Date recorded: 2026-11-04
Owner approval: Stated by the owner in the project hand-off log; the original date of the statement is not recorded here. Seeded into this record on 2026-11-04.

## Chosen
The GitHub Pages prototype (`https://save-the-lives.github.io/cevoc-prototype`, built from `prototype/` by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) keeps its URL. New work does not move or rename it.

## Why
People already use these links.

## Ruled out
Renaming the repository or Pages site; moving the prototype to a new path.
